MY EXPERIENCE

What Happened?

During the Diabetes Awareness Workshop, we were introduced to diabetes and learned about its impact on people's health and everyday lives. The session helped us understand why diabetes awareness is important and why everyone should have a basic knowledge of this health condition.

We learned about the different types of diabetes, common symptoms, possible risk factors and the importance of early detection. The session also highlighted how healthy eating habits, regular physical activity and maintaining a healthy lifestyle can help reduce the risk of Type 2 diabetes.

It was an informative experience that gave us an opportunity to learn more about diabetes, clear up common misconceptions and understand the importance of spreading awareness within our families and communities.

What I Learned

This workshop helped me understand that diabetes is a serious health condition that requires awareness, attention and proper management. I learned to recognise common warning signs and appreciate the importance of healthy lifestyle choices and regular health check-ups. Most importantly, I realised that sharing accurate information and educating others can help create a more health-conscious community.
