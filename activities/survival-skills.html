MY EXPERIENCE

What Happened?

During the Survival Skills activity, each group was given a different survival situation to solve. Our group was assigned a challenging scenario where we had to imagine ourselves stranded at a blizzard camp near Mount Everest.

We were given a list of items and had to decide which seven items would be the most important for our survival. This required us to discuss different possibilities, consider the risks of the extreme weather conditions and agree on which resources would be most useful.

It was challenging because we had to make difficult choices with limited resources. We shared our opinions, discussed our priorities and worked together to select our final seven items.

TEAM PARTICIPATION

What I Learned

This activity taught me the importance of decision-making, teamwork and staying calm in difficult situations. I learned that survival is not only about having the right resources but also about knowing how to prioritise, adapt and make responsible decisions with what is available. It also helped me understand how important cooperation and clear communication are when facing unexpected challenges.
