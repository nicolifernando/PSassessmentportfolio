What is Personal Branding?

Personal branding is the intentional and consistent way you communicate your skills, values, strengths, personality and professional identity so that others understand what you offer and what makes you unique.

◎

Why is Personal Branding Important?

  • Helps you build a clear professional identity
  • Communicates your value to others
  • Creates credibility and trust
  • Increases your visibility and opportunities
  • Supports networking and career growth
  • Helps you stay consistent across platforms
⚙

Key Elements of a Personal Brand

Your personal brand is built through a combination of:

01 Identity / Purpose Who you are and what you stand for
02 Strengths & Skills What you are good at
03 Values What drives your decisions
04 Target Audience Who you want to reach (e.g. employers, peers)
05 Communication Style How you present yourself (online & offline)
06 Online Presence Your digital footprint (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, etc.)
07 Consistency Maintaining a unified and authentic image
♧

Build Your Personal Brand

A STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

01 Know Yourself Identify your passions, strengths and goals.
02 Define Your Value Understand what you offer and what makes you different.
03 Build Your Presence Create and optimise your online profiles.
04 Share Your Work Showcase your projects, skills and achievements.
05 Review & Improve Keep learning, seek feedback and update regularly.

A SIMPLE FRAMEWORK

e.g. a passionate Computer Science undergraduate

D
What do I do?

e.g. develop innovative solutions, focus on cybersecurity and software development

V
What value do I bring?

e.g. problem-solving, creativity, dedication

G

e.g. to grow in tech, create a positive impact and keep learning

Common Mistakes to Avoid

BE MINDFUL AND DO BETTER

× Being inconsistent across platforms
× Using an unprofessional profile or username
× Not showcasing your achievements
× Copying others instead of being authentic
× Having an incomplete LinkedIn profile
× Focusing only on technical skills
× Ignoring networking and engagement
⚙

Quick Tips

SMALL ACTIONS LEAD TO BIG RESULTS

✓ Be authentic and true to yourself
✓ Keep your profiles updated
✓ Use a professional and consistent visual style
✓ Share your journey, not just your achievements
✓ Engage with others in your field
✓ Be open to opportunities and collaborations
✓ Remember: your personal brand grows over time
