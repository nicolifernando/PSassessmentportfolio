What is Communication Etiquette?

Communication etiquette refers to the professional manners and behaviours we use when communicating with others. It helps us communicate clearly, respectfully and appropriately in academic and workplace settings.

Professional communication is about both what you say and how you say it.
◎

Why is it Important?

01 Creates a professional impression
02 Builds trust and respect
03 Reduces misunderstandings
04 Improves relationships
05 Makes communication more effective
☎

Telephone Etiquette

Telephone etiquette involves using professional, respectful and effective behaviours when making or receiving a phone call.

Before the Call

  • Know the purpose of the call
  • Prepare important information
  • Choose an appropriate time
  • Find a quiet environment

During the Call

  • Greet the person politely
  • Introduce yourself clearly
  • Speak clearly and confidently
  • Listen without interrupting

Professional Behaviour

  • Use a respectful tone
  • Stay focused on the conversation
  • Take notes when necessary
  • Clarify important information

Ending the Call

  • Summarise important points
  • Confirm the next step
  • Thank the person
  • End the call politely
@

Email Etiquette

Email etiquette refers to the professional standards used when writing, sending and responding to emails.

01 Subject Line — Clearly state the purpose
02 Greeting — Use an appropriate greeting
03 Opening — Explain why you are writing
04 Main Message — Keep information clear
05 Closing — End politely and professionally
✉

Professional Email

Subject: Request for Assignment Extension

Dear Professor,

I am writing to kindly request an extension for the assignment due to unforeseen circumstances.

I would appreciate your consideration of my request.

Kind regards,
Nicoli Fernando
!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

× Using informal language in professional situations
× Speaking without listening
× Sending emails without proofreading
× Forgetting attachments
× Using an unclear subject line
× Responding emotionally or impulsively

Telephone vs Email

Choosing the right communication method is also an important professional skill.

Telephone Email
Immediate communication Asynchronous communication
Useful for urgent matters Useful for detailed information
Allows immediate clarification Creates a written record
Tone of voice matters Written tone matters
