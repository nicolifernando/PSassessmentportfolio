What is a Job Interview?

A job interview is a conversation between a job applicant and a representative of an organisation that is looking to hire. Both parties exchange information and evidence to determine whether the job is a suitable match.

◎

Develop Your Interview Strategy

Prepare yourself by answering three important questions:

01. What do I want?

What should the interviewer know about me?

02. What should I improve?

What weaknesses or disadvantages should I minimise?

03. What do I need to know?

What should I learn about the job and organisation?

◇

The Role of Effective Communication

Communication skills are essential because your responses and behaviour demonstrate your attitude, personality and professionalism.

Verbal Communication

  • Speak clearly and confidently.
  • Use industry terms and technical vocabulary.
  • Provide specific answers and examples.
  • Respond positively to changes in topics.
  • Ask questions where appropriate.
  • Show enthusiasm when discussing the role.

Non-Verbal Communication

  • Smile and greet the interviewer.
  • Maintain comfortable eye contact.
  • Sit firmly and maintain good posture.
  • Show confidence through body language.
  • Pay attention to the interviewer's cues.
  • Demonstrate enthusiasm and interest.

The 7–38–55 Communication Model

7% Words
38% Tone of Voice
55% Body Language

According to the lecture, these figures illustrate the importance of non-verbal communication.

Active Listening

Listening carefully helps you understand questions, respond appropriately and build better communication.

01 Pay attention.
02 Show that you are listening.
03 Seek clarification when necessary.
04 Defer judgement.
05 Respond appropriately.

Successful vs Unsuccessful Candidates

✓ Successful: Specific and consistent answers.
✓ Successful: Researched the company.
✓ Successful: Maintained eye contact and smiled.
✓ Successful: Used examples and technical terms.
× Unsuccessful: Gave vague and short answers.
× Unsuccessful: Showed little eye contact.
× Unsuccessful: Asked very few questions.
✦

The 6 C's of Effective Communication

Apply these principles to make your answers professional and effective.

01. Clear

Present information in an understandable way.

02. Concise

Keep answers focused without unnecessary details.

03. Correct

Provide accurate and truthful information.

04. Complete

Include all the necessary information.

05. Concrete

Support your answers with examples and evidence.

06. Courteous

Always communicate politely and respectfully.

Remember: Confidence matters too!

Demonstrate a confident tone and a positive "can-do" attitude.

Structure of an Interview

A typical interview follows four main stages.

01. Introduction

Greetings, introductions and initial conversation.

02. Evidence-Speaking

Discuss your qualifications, skills and experiences.

03. Your Questions

Ask relevant questions about the position and organisation.

04. Closure

End the interview politely and professionally.

☆

The STAR Method

A useful technique for structuring interview answers.

S

Situation

Describe the situation and background.

T

Task

Explain what you needed to achieve.

A

Action

Describe the actions you personally took.

R

Result

Explain the outcome of your actions.

Example Interview Question

Tell us about a challenge you faced and how you handled it.

Situation: A team was behind schedule on an important project.

Task: The project needed to be completed before the deadline.

Action: Tasks were delegated according to team members' strengths and responsibilities were reorganised.

Result: The project was completed on time and the experience improved leadership skills.

Before the Interview

Pay attention to these important practical details.

01 What to wear: Choose appropriate and professional attire.

Professional Interview Attire

Your appearance contributes to the professional impression you create.

✓ Wear clean, neat and well-fitted clothing.
✓ Choose business casual or business professional attire.
✓ Keep accessories simple and appropriate.
✓ Ensure your hair and shoes are well-groomed.
× Avoid overly casual, distracting or untidy clothing.
✦

Mock Interviews

Practise before the real interview!

What is a Mock Interview?

A mock interview is a practice interview designed to simulate a real job interview. It allows candidates to practise answering questions and improve their communication and presentation skills.

Why are They Useful?

They help you become more confident, identify areas for improvement, practise the STAR method and become familiar with the interview environment.
