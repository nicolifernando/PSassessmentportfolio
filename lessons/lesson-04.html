What is a CV?

A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a formal document that summarises your education, skills, experience and achievements. It gives potential employers an overview of who you are, what you can do and why you are a good fit for a role.

◎

Why is a CV Important?

  • Creates a strong first impression
  • Helps you stand out from other applicants
  • Showcases your skills and achievements
  • Opens doors to interviews and opportunities
  • Reflects your professionalism and attention to detail
▤

Key Sections of a CV

A well-structured CV usually includes:

01 Personal Information (name, contact details, LinkedIn)
02 Professional Summary / Objective
03 Education
04 Work Experience (internships, part-time, projects)
05 Skills (technical and soft skills)
06 Achievements & Certifications
07 Additional Sections (volunteering, extracurricular, etc.)
◇

Qualities of a Good CV

An effective CV should be:

✓ Clear and well-organised
✓ Concise and relevant
✓ Tailored to the job role
✓ Error-free (grammar and spelling)
✓ Visually clean and professional
✓ Highlights your strengths and achievements
✓ Updated regularly
⚙

Types of CVs

The type of CV you use may depend on your experience and the role you are applying for.

Chronological CV

Focuses on work history (in most recent order).

Functional CV

Focuses on skills and abilities.

Combination CV

A mix of skills and work experience.

Academic CV

Used for academic and research roles.

!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

STEER CLEAR FOR A STRONGER CV

× Spelling and grammatical errors
× Including irrelevant or outdated information
× Using an unprofessional email address
× Writing long, unclear paragraphs
× Not quantifying achievements (e.g. "Managed a team" instead of "Managed a team and increased sales by 20%")
× Using the same CV for every application
× Poor formatting or cluttered design
× Exaggerating or providing false information
⚙

Tips for Tailoring Your CV

MAKE IT RELEVANT AND IMPACTFUL

✓ Read the job description carefully
✓ Highlight relevant skills and experiences
✓ Use keywords from the job posting
✓ Adjust your professional summary/objective
✓ Showcase achievements that match the role
✓ Keep the format clean and consistent
✓ Remove irrelevant information
✓ Tailor your CV for each application
