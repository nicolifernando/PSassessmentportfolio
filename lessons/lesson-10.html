Grooming Etiquette

PRESENT   •   MAINTAIN   •   IMPRESS

10LESSON
✧

What is Grooming Etiquette?

Grooming etiquette refers to the habits and practices that help a person maintain a clean, neat and appropriate appearance. It includes personal hygiene, clothing, hair care and overall presentation in social and professional settings.

◎

Why Does Grooming Matter?

  • Creates a positive first impression
  • Shows self-respect and confidence
  • Demonstrates professionalism
  • Supports personal hygiene and wellbeing
  • Helps others feel comfortable around you
♡

Essential Grooming Habits

Simple daily habits make a noticeable difference.

01Personal hygieneBathe regularly and use deodorant when needed.
02Oral careBrush and floss; keep your breath fresh.
03Hair careKeep hair clean, tidy and manageable.
04Nail careKeep fingernails and toenails clean and neat.
05Skin careMaintain a simple, suitable skincare routine.
✓

Personal Presentation

THE DETAILS THAT COMPLETE YOUR LOOK

01ClothingWear clean, ironed and well-fitting clothes.
02FootwearChoose clean and appropriate shoes.
03AccessoriesKeep accessories simple and suitable.
04FragranceUse a light amount; avoid overpowering scents.
05PostureStand confidently and maintain good posture.
✦

Grooming in Everyday Life

Good grooming is not about expensive products or following every trend. It is about being clean, comfortable, prepared and appropriate for the occasion.

University and classes Job interviews Workplace meetings Social events
♡

Personal Hygiene Essentials

CLEANLINESS IS THE FOUNDATION

01
Daily cleanlinessMaintain regular bathing and wear fresh clothes.
02
Hand hygieneWash hands regularly, especially before eating.
03
Oral hygieneBrush twice daily and maintain fresh breath.
04
Hair and nailsKeep them clean, trimmed and well maintained.
05
Clothing careChange clothes regularly and avoid visible stains.
▣

Professional Grooming

LOOK APPROPRIATE FOR THE SETTING

01
Follow the dress codeUnderstand the expectations of your workplace or event.
02
Choose suitable clothingWear clean, comfortable and well-fitting outfits.
03
Keep a neat appearanceCheck your hair, clothes and footwear before leaving.
04
Be considerateAvoid strong fragrances and distracting accessories.
05
Stay preparedCarry basic personal-care essentials when necessary.
◇

Dressing for the Occasion

CLOTHING SHOULD MATCH THE CONTEXT

01
FormalStructured, neat clothing for interviews and formal meetings.
02
Business casualSmart trousers, shirts, blouses or modest dresses.
03
CasualComfortable, clean clothing suitable for the activity.
04
Special occasionsRespect the event's dress expectations.
!

Common Grooming Mistakes

SMALL THINGS THAT CAN AFFECT IMPRESSIONS

×Wearing wrinkled or unclean clothes
×Ignoring oral or personal hygiene
×Using too much perfume or cologne
×Wearing unsuitable clothing for the occasion
×Neglecting hair, nails or footwear
×Assuming grooming is only about appearance
✓

A Simple Daily Grooming Routine

A QUICK CHECK BEFORE YOU STEP OUT

✓Freshen up and use clean clothes.
✓Brush your teeth and check your breath.
✓Neaten your hair and check your nails.
✓Check that your outfit is clean and suitable.
✓Use fragrance lightly, if you choose to.
✓Check your overall appearance before leaving.
