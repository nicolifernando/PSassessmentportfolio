What is Dining Etiquette?

Dining is more than just eating. It is a social experience that reflects who we are. Good dining etiquette shows respect for others, creates a positive impression and helps us feel comfortable in different social and professional settings.

The way we behave at the table can influence how others perceive us.
◎

Why is Dining Etiquette Important?

01 Creates a good first impression
02 Shows respect for others
03 Helps social situations feel smoother
04 Builds discipline and confidence
05 Reflects personality and values
06 Supports professional relationships
♧

The Basics of Table Etiquette

Small details at the table can make a big difference in creating a respectful and comfortable dining experience.

Before the Meal

  • Wait until everyone is seated
  • Place your napkin appropriately
  • Keep your phone away from the table
  • Wait for the host before beginning

At the Table

  • Sit upright and maintain good posture
  • Keep elbows off the table while eating
  • Use utensils appropriately
  • Chew with your mouth closed

Conversation

  • Be respectful and polite
  • Allow others to speak
  • Avoid talking with food in your mouth
  • Keep conversation appropriate

General Courtesy

  • Thank the host
  • Be considerate of others
  • Avoid unnecessary noise
  • Follow the atmosphere of the setting
♢

How to Use Cutlery

Knowing where each utensil belongs helps create a confident and organised dining experience.

01 Forks are placed on the left.
02 Knives are placed on the right.
03 Spoons are placed on the right.
04 Use utensils from the outside towards the plate.
05 Place utensils neatly on the plate when finished.
◉

During the Meal

01 Cut food into small pieces at a time.
02 Keep your fork and knife on the plate when pausing.
03 Do not put too much food in your mouth.
04 Eat at a comfortable pace.
05 Avoid reaching across other people.
♧

Restaurant Etiquette

01 Be respectful and polite to restaurant staff.
02 Follow the restaurant's atmosphere and expectations.
03 Avoid speaking loudly or disturbing other diners.
04 Use your phone discreetly.
05 Thank staff for their service.
◎

Dining in Professional Settings

01 Arrive on time.
02 Dress appropriately for the occasion.
03 Maintain professional conversation.
04 Avoid controversial or uncomfortable topics.
05 Show appreciation to your host.
